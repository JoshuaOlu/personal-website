A deeply human story, told through an email exchange between a young Nigerian engineering student and a professor overseas.

On the surface, it is about ethics in engineering education. But beneath that, it explores the quiet struggles many people in developing nations face in academic and professional spaces, struggles we rarely talk about openly.

The story is fictional, yet it is inspired by reality. My hope is that these letters spark honest conversations about the challenges around technological dependence, educational inequities, and the ethical dilemmas many students navigate every day.

Each letter was first published weekly on Substack, from November 2025 to February 2026. They are best read in order.
